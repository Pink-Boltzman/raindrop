Current Research

Near-Field Quantum Imaging

My current research focuses on phase-substructure in near-field quantum imaging systems. I study spatial correlations of photon pairs generated through SPDC and analyze how alignment conditions affect interference and detection probabilities.

Spontaneous Parametric Down-Conversion (SPDC)

I investigate the structure of the biphoton probability amplitude, decomposing it into angular pump spectrum and phase-matching contributions. My work involves dimensional analysis, Fourier-space modeling, and quantum field operator formalism.

Quantum Foundations

I am also interested in conceptual aspects of quantum mechanics, including entanglement, Bell inequalities, density matrices, and the relationship between unitary evolution and information.
